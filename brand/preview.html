Colour, type and icons for the OpenHamShack platform, its apps and OpenHamShack Remote. Everything on this page is generated from the token and glyph sources in brand/; nothing is fetched.

01

Mark and wordmark

A shack whose roofline carries on past the ridge as an aerial, aimed up and right with its element at the end. The open floor is the open in the name. The aerial makes the same gesture as the dish and the Yagi in the app icons, so the mark and the apps read as one family. The wordmark is outlined from Lato (SIL OFL): Open in Medium, HamShack in Heavy, so the name still parses in one colour.

One colour
Mark, 16 to 96 px (heavier drawing at 24 px and below)

Clear space and minimum size

2u2u

u is the diameter of the aerial's element, the dot. Keep 2u clear on every side of a lockup, and 1u round the mark alone.

Minimum: horizontal lockup 120 px wide (24 mm in print), stacked 64 px, wordmark 80 px. Below that use the mark: the small drawing from 24 px down to 16 px.

Never: swap the two colours, set the name in another face, stretch, outline, add a shadow or glow, put the mark on a busy photo, or abbreviate the name to its initials.

Rules of thumb

Red means transmit

Never use the tx colour for errors, deletes or decoration. An error is warn or ink.

Colour plus words

Every state also says itself: ON AIR, LOCKED. Colour alone fails for colour-blind operators and on a washed-out TV.

Digits don't move

Frequencies, meters and timers use the mono stack or tabular figures.

Tokens, not hex

Use --shack-*, @color/shack_* or Shack.*. A hex value in product code is a bug.
